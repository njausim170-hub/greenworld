Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Green World Health Solutions collects and uses your information when you use this website.

Information we collect

We collect the details you provide when you register, place an order, request a consultation or contact us — such as your name, phone number, email, delivery address and any information you choose to share about your health concern.

How we use it

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To respond to consultation requests and enquiries.
  • To provide customer support and, where you have opted in, wellness updates.

Sensitive information

Health information you submit through a consultation request is treated as confidential, stored securely and used only to respond to your request. We do not publish it, and we do not sell your personal data.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us. You can unsubscribe from marketing messages at any time.

This is a starter policy. Please review and adapt it to your business and applicable Kenyan data-protection law before going live.
