Shipping & Delivery

We deliver health and wellness products countrywide across Kenya through trusted couriers.

Delivery timelines

  • Nairobi: same or next business day for orders placed before mid-afternoon.
  • Countrywide: typically 1-4 business days depending on your location.

Delivery charges

Delivery charges are calculated at checkout based on your location and order. Configure your rates under WooCommerce > Settings > Shipping.

Order processing

You will receive updates as your order is confirmed and dispatched. Track progress any time on our Track Your Order page.

Payment

Pay securely with M-Pesa, card or on delivery where available.
